# IEBC Academy: Course Catalog and Curriculum

Practical AI and automation classes taught by IEBC consultants. Every class ends with
something working in the student's business. Prices are starting suggestions; adjust in
`index.html` (`ACADEMY_COURSES`).

## How enrollment works

1. The student picks a class on the Academy page and submits the enrollment form (no payment taken).
2. The enrollment goes to the hub pipeline as a prospect (`source: academy_enrollment`,
   industry `Academy`) and appears in hub activity as "New Academy enrollment".
3. An IEBC consultant emails within one business day with a payment link, start date and access.
4. Policy shown on the site: the paid course fee is credited toward any IEBC build within 90 days.
